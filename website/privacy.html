Overview

TallyFin (“we”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy describes how we handle information when you use our desktop agent, mobile application, and related services.

Data we process

When you sync Tally data, business information such as ledgers, vouchers, and inventory may be transmitted to our secure servers for the purpose of providing the service to your authorized users and devices.

How we use data

  • To sync and display your accounting data on authorized devices
  • To provide support and improve the product
  • To process subscriptions and comply with law

Security

We use encryption in transit and access controls. Only users you authorize can access your organization’s data.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of account data by contacting us. Synced accounting data can be removed by revoking devices and closing your account.
