Why TallyFin

Your entire business, beautifully on mobile

Everything your TallyPrime knows — every company, ledger, voucher and stock item — organised into clear, live mobile dashboards.

All companies, one login

Switch between every company in your TallyPrime instantly. Group companies, compare performance and see consolidated numbers at a glance.

Real-time sync

The TallyFin desktop agent syncs changes from TallyPrime within seconds — vouchers, ledgers, masters and stock — automatically and securely.

Live financial reports

P&L, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance, Cash & Bank Book, Day Book and more — rendered for mobile, filterable by any period.

Outstanding & ageing

Know exactly who owes you and for how long. Receivables and payables with bill-wise ageing, ready for your next follow-up call.

Inventory on hand

Live stock summary with quantities, rates and values across godowns. Spot fast-movers and dead stock before they cost you.

Private & secure

End-to-end encrypted transport, read-only access and role-based permissions. Your books never leave your control.
